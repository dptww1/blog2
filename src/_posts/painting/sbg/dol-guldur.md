<p>Adapted from the painting guide in the <i>Fall of the Necromancer</i> (2021) supplement.</p>

<h3>Entire Model</h3>

<ol>
<li>Overall basecoat in Rhinox Hide (=> 2:1 Craftsman Brown:Americana Lamp Black)</li>
<li>Heavy drybrush Eshin Grey (=> 3:2 Americana Lamp Black:Folk Art Titanium White)</li>
<li>Drybrush Mechanicus Standard Grey (=> 2:3 Americana Lamp Black:Folk Art Titanium White)</li>
<li>Very light drybrush Dawnstone</li>
<li>Dab green in various places (1:1 GW Biel-tan Green:GW Yellow Ink)</li>
</ol>

<h3>The Metal Bits</h3>

<ul>
<li>basecoat GW Warplock Bronze</li>
<li>very heavy drybrush Army War Painter Gun Metal</li>
</ul>

<p>Wash entire model in GW Nuln Oil</p>

<p>Light drybrush the metal bits with Army War Painter Plate Mail</p>

<h3>Vines</h3>

<ul>
<li>Basecoat with Vallejo Russian Uniform WWII</li>
<li>Heavy drybrush Militarum Green (Contrast)</li>
<li>Drybrush Vallejo Russian Uniform WWII</li>
<li>Light drybrush GW Straken Green</li>
<li>Highlight GW Elysian Green</li>
</ul>

<h3>Dead Trees</h3>

<ol>
<li>Basecoat with GW Rhinox Hide</li>
<li>Heavy drybrush with GW Gorthor Brown</li>
<li>Drybrush with GW Baneblade Brown</li>
<li>Light drybrush with GW Karak Stone</li>
</ol>

<h3>Bones &amp; Skulls</h3>

<ol>
<li>Basecoat with GW Karak Stone</li>
<li>Wash with Agrax Earthshade</li>
<li>Heavy drybrush GW Screaming Skull</li>
<li>Highlight with 1:1 Vallejo White:Water</li>
</ol>
